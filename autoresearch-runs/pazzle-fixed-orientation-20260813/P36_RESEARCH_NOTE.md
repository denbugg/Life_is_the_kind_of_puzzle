Graph Connection Laplacian results also motivate robustness to corruption in an uncertain connection graph. GANzzle++ provides a complementary global-to-local formulation: reconstruct a joint latent solution image and solve a one-to-one assignment, but P35 evidence rules out tile-only absolute placement without image-specific relational evidence.`n`nP34 already falsified binary 2×2 witness selection: mutual-edge coverage fell from 60.180782% to 52.748528%. P36 must therefore preserve every baseline candidate and add only a small, confidence-weighted 2×2 path-consistency score.
